## INSTAGRAM

### Why your accounts keep getting banned
Meta's AI flags the word "peptide" in usernames and bios before you even post.
It also links bans to your device, phone number, and IP. If you create a new
account on the same phone/wifi as a banned account, it gets flagged instantly.

### How to create an account that survives

**Device setup (do this once):**
- Use a phone that has NEVER had a banned account on it
- Use a fresh SIM card (local Indonesian SIM)
- Create a new Gmail or ProtonMail address — never reuse old email
- Create the account on 4G mobile data, NOT your usual WiFi
- Do NOT use a VPN during setup

**Username — never use these words:**
- peptide / peptides
- BPC, TB-500, semaglutide, tirzepatide, GHK, NAD
- research chem / research chemical
- injectable / injection
- supplement + any drug word

**Safe username options:**
- @axiombali
- @axiom.optimize
- @axiom_longevity
- @axiomvitality
- @bali.biohack
- @axiom.wellness.lab

**Bio copy (paste exactly — no peptide words):**
```
Optimizing human performance from Bali 🌴
Science-backed longevity & biohacking protocols
Explore our research catalog via link below
For enquiries: [your email or WhatsApp]
```

**Account setup rules:**
- Choose category: "Personal Blog" or "Local Business" — NOT Health/Wellness
- Do NOT add a product link in bio for the first 14 days
- Post 10-15 lifestyle/education posts before any product reference
- Never name specific compounds in captions
- Use code words the community knows: "compounds," "protocols," "optimization tools," "our catalog"
- Banned hashtags to avoid: #peptides #BPC157 #researchchemicals
- Safe hashtags: #biohacking #longevity #humanoptimization #performancelab #balilife #wellnessbali
