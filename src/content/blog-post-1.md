## What Are Research Peptides?

Research peptides are short chains of amino acids used in scientific study. They are sold strictly for in-vitro and laboratory research purposes. Common compounds studied include BPC-157, TB-500, Semaglutide, Tirzepatide, GHK-Cu, and NAD+, each with a growing body of published research across platforms like PubMed.

A legitimate research supplier will provide a third-party Certificate of Analysis for every batch. A COA confirms purity percentage, compound identity, and the absence of contaminants. Never purchase from a supplier who cannot produce a COA on request.

Research-grade peptides should be a minimum of 98% purity, with premium suppliers hitting 99%+. The purity level directly affects the reliability of your research data.

Peptides are sensitive to heat, light, and improper handling. Bali's tropical climate makes storage practices especially important. Ask your supplier how compounds are stored before shipping.

### 4. Transparent Pricing
Pricing in the research peptide space varies widely. Be cautious of unusually cheap sources — low price often signals low purity or misrepresented compounds. Equally, premium pricing doesn't guarantee quality without a COA to back it up.
